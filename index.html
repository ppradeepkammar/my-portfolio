About Me

I'm Pradeep Kammar, a B.E. student in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Tontadarya College of Engineering, Gadag, with a strong interest in Embedded Systems, IoT, Robotics, Automation, and sensor-based applications.

I enjoy working with both hardware and software to develop practical solutions for real-world problems. I have experience working with platforms such as Arduino UNO, ESP32, and STM32, along with technologies including Embedded C, C, Python, SQL, and HTML.

I have worked on projects such as a Smart Emergency Alertness System and ESP32-Based Bluetooth Communication, where I explored embedded hardware, sensors, wireless communication, and microcontroller programming.

I am particularly interested in hardware debugging, learning new technologies, teamwork, and creative problem-solving. My goal is to continuously improve my technical skills and build a career as an Embedded Systems Engineer.
